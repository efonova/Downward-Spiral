Sara Lee ran successive workforce reductions over more than a decade. One program cut 8,000–9,000 jobs (about 6% of the then workforce). In one 18-month window cumulative announced reductions reached 14,263. In a later multi-year phase the company cut its workforce by 62%, from 137,000 employees in 2005 to 52,300. Plants closed and further rounds continued. The company steadily shrank

Circuit City terminated 3,400 store employees in March 2007 — roughly 8–9% of its ~40,000 in-store workforce — because their pay exceeded internal ranges. The company projected $110 million in expense reductions that fiscal year and $140 million annually thereafter. Sales of complex items (especially large televisions) then fell substantially below plan. The company projected an $80–90 million first-quarter loss. It filed for bankruptcy in November 2008 and was liquidated. An earlier 2003 move had already removed nearly 3,900 commissioned salespeople.

Kraft Heinz recorded a $15.4 billion impairment on goodwill and intangible assets in 2019. Analyses linked this to the preceding years of aggressive cuts to overhead, marketing, and related spending. Gross margin declined by approximately 3.5 percentage points in the same period while sales remained flat or declined in real terms.

France Télécom's aggressive workforce-reduction program was linked to at least 19 employee suicides, 12 attempts, and multiple cases of serious depression. Earlier schemes had already reduced the workforce by roughly one-quarter from a base of about 162,500.
